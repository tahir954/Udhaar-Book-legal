Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What data this app stores

Udhaar Book stores all your business data — customers, suppliers, ledger entries, cash book entries, and bills — locally on your device. This data is never sent to us or any third party.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google and use the backup feature, the app requests access only to files it creates itself in your Google Drive, using Google's restricted drive.file scope. This means:

  • The app can only see and modify the single backup file it creates — never any other file in your Drive.
  • Your backup data (a JSON file containing your ledger/cash book/bill records) is stored in your own Google Drive account, under your control.
  • We do not have access to your Google Drive, your Google account, or any data within it beyond that single backup file.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transmit your business data to any third party. The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing (if applicable).
